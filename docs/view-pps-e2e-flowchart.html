End-to-End Production Planning & Scheduling

Role-based flowchart — from asset availability through shift assignment, leave, overtime, machine breakdown, out-source (e.g. heat treatment), shop floor, dynamic replanning, to part completed. Manufacturing language only.

Download .mmd Open Mermaid Live Other manufacturing diagrams
Administrator Manufacturing Coordinator Supervisor Operator
Flow covers: Asset availability · Breakdown notice (operator → admin/coordinator) · Machine return & resume · Shift assignment · Overtime · Operator leave (blocks job card) · Order & part ready · Baseline & live plan · In-house vs out-source (heat treatment) · Job card cycle · Dynamic reschedule triggers · Priority swap · Part completed 
flowchart TB

    START([Start — Plant ready for production planning])

    subgraph ASSETS["1 — Asset availability"]
        direction TB
        A1[Administrator / Coordinator
Review machines available for work]
        A2{Is every machine
fit for production?}
        A3[Administrator / Coordinator
Mark machine available]
        A4[Administrator / Coordinator
Mark machine unavailable
breakdown start and expected return]
        A1 --> A2
        A2 -->|Yes| A3
        A2 -->|No — breakdown / maintenance| A4
    end

    START --> A1

    subgraph BD_NOTIFY["Breakdown notice from the floor"]
        direction TB
        N1[Operator notices machine failed]
        N2[Operator notifies Administrator
or Manufacturing Coordinator]
        N3[Administrator / Coordinator
Records breakdown period
machine off from date — to date]
        N4[Live schedule recalculates
no new work on that machine
during unavailable window]
        N1 --> N2 --> N3 --> N4
    end

    A4 -.-> N3
    N1 -.-> A2

    subgraph BD_RESUME["After machine returns to service"]
        direction TB
        R1[Administrator / Coordinator
Marks machine available again]
        R2[Interrupted job resumes
remaining machining time continues
setup is not repeated]
        R3[Next jobs run only after
earlier operations on that part
are finished]
        R4[Other parts competing for
the same machine are pushed
to realistic new times]
        R1 --> R2 --> R3 --> R4
    end

    A3 --> SHIFT
    N4 --> R1
    R4 --> SHIFT

    subgraph SHIFT["2 — Shift planning and machine–operator assignment"]
        direction TB
        S1[Administrator
Set working calendar and shift hours]
        S2{Overtime needed
for this day?}
        S3[Administrator
Extend shift / record overtime hours]
        S4[Administrator
Assign operator to machine
for that shift day]
        S5[Operator sees assignment
on job board]
        S1 --> S2
        S2 -->|Yes| S3
        S2 -->|No — normal shift| S4
        S3 --> S4
        S4 --> S5
    end

    subgraph LEAVE["3 — Operator leave"]
        direction TB
        L1[Operator requests leave]
        L2[Administrator / Supervisor / Coordinator
Approves leave]
        L3[Leave acknowledged
for the leave dates]
        L4{Job card start
on a leave day?}
        L5[Start blocked
Operator cannot activate job card
until leave dates are over]
        L6[Operator may start jobs
on non-leave days only]
        L1 --> L2 --> L3 --> L4
        L4 -->|Yes — on leave| L5
        L4 -->|No — available| L6
    end

    S5 --> LEAVE_CHK{Is operator on
approved leave today?}
    LEAVE_CHK -->|Yes| L5
    LEAVE_CHK -->|No| READY_ORD
    L1 -.-> LEAVE_CHK

    subgraph READY_ORD["4 — Order and part ready for scheduling"]
        direction TB
        O1[Administrator
Activate sale order]
        O2{Raw material linked
and drawing ready?}
        O3[Administrator
Activate in-house part]
        O4[Manufacturing Coordinator
Set part priority — who runs first]
        O5[Order and part enter
the scheduling pool]
        O1 --> O2
        O2 -->|No| O2B[Hold — cannot schedule yet]
        O2 -->|Yes| O3 --> O4 --> O5
    end

    subgraph PLAN["5 — Create the production plan"]
        direction TB
        P1[Administrator / Coordinator
Generate baseline schedule]
        P2[Plan respects
priority · shift hours · machine availability]
        P3[Copy plan to live schedule
shown on Gantt and job cards]
        P1 --> P2 --> P3
    end

    O5 --> P1

    subgraph ROUTING["6 — Operation type on the part route"]
        direction TB
        RT1{Next operation
on this part?}
        RT2{Made on plant machine
or sent outside?}
        RT3[In-house operation
Machining on assigned machine]
        RT4[Out-source operation
e.g. heat treatment]
        RT5[Administrator / Coordinator
Record sent to vendor]
        RT6{Returned from vendor?}
        RT7[Record delivered
Downstream in-house ops
may start after delivery]
        RT8[Hold next in-house work
until out-source returns]
        RT1 --> RT2
        RT2 -->|In-house| RT3
        RT2 -->|Out-source| RT4
        RT4 --> RT5 --> RT6
        RT6 -->|Yes| RT7
        RT6 -->|No| RT8
        RT8 --> RT6
    end

    P3 --> RT1
    RT3 --> SHOP
    RT7 --> RT1

    subgraph SHOP["7 — Shop floor — job card cycle"]
        direction TB
        J1[Operator opens job card
sees live start and finish]
        J2{All start checks pass?}
        J3[Operator starts the job]
        J4[Operator records what was made
new manufacture and rework]
        J5[Operator submits for approval]
        J6[Supervisor or Coordinator reviews
approve · rework · reject]
        J7{More work still needed
on this operation?}
        J2 -->|No| J2B[Blocked — see blocking reasons]
        J2 -->|Yes| J3 --> J4 --> J5 --> J6 --> J7
        J7 -->|Yes| J1
        J7 -->|No — this op done| NEXT_OP
    end

    subgraph BLOCKS["When start is blocked"]
        direction LR
        B1[Machine unavailable]
        B2[Before scheduled start]
        B3[Earlier operation not finished]
        B4[Previous submission
awaiting review]
        B5[Operator on leave]
        B6[Nothing left to make]
    end

    J2B --- B1
    J2B --- B2
    J2B --- B3
    J2B --- B4
    J2B --- B5
    J2B --- B6

    NEXT_OP{More operations
on this part?}
    NEXT_OP -->|Yes| RT1
    NEXT_OP -->|No| COMPLETE

    subgraph DYNAMIC["8 — Dynamic scheduling — live plan stays honest"]
        direction TB
        D0[Live schedule recalculates when…]
        D1[Production approved / reworked / rejected]
        D2[Machine marked unavailable or back]
        D3[Part priority swapped]
        D4[Out-source delivery recorded]
        D5[Shift / overtime window changed]
        D6[Outcomes]
        D7[Later jobs get new start and finish times]
        D8[Work after breakdown resumes remaining time only]
        D9[In-house work after heat treatment
waits for vendor return]
        D10[Higher-priority parts take machines first]
        D0 --- D1
        D0 --- D2
        D0 --- D3
        D0 --- D4
        D0 --- D5
        D1 --> D6
        D2 --> D6
        D3 --> D6
        D4 --> D6
        D5 --> D6
        D6 --> D7
        D6 --> D8
        D6 --> D9
        D6 --> D10
    end

    J6 -.-> D1
    N3 -.-> D2
    R1 -.-> D2
    O4 -.-> D3
    RT7 -.-> D4
    S3 -.-> D5
    D7 -.-> J1

    subgraph PRIORITY["9 — Priority change under pressure"]
        direction TB
        PR1[Manufacturing Coordinator
Customer expedites a part]
        PR2[Preview impact on machines and due dates]
        PR3[Confirm swap]
        PR4[Live schedule recalculates globally]
        PR1 --> PR2 --> PR3 --> PR4
    end

    PR4 -.-> D3

    subgraph COMPLETE["10 — Part completed"]
        direction TB
        C1[Every operation approved]
        C2[Part status marked completed]
        C3[Part removed from priority queue]
        C4[Next parts move up]
        C1 --> C2 --> C3 --> C4
    end

    C4 --> END([End — Part finished in Production Planning and Scheduling])
